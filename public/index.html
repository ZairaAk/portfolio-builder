Process

Three steps to your
perfect portfolio

01

Create your account

Sign up in seconds. Choose a unique username that becomes your portfolio URL — like folio.app/u/yourname.

02

Add your content

Fill in your projects, skills, work experience, and education. Upload images, add links — everything you need.

03

Publish & share

Pick a template, toggle publish, and share your link. Track how many people view your portfolio in real time.
